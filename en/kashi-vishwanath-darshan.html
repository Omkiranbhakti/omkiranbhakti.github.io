Kashi Vishwanath Darshan Guide: sugam darshan, Mangla aarti and rules

The Kashi Vishwanath temple is open from 3 AM to 11 PM and general darshan is free. For darshan without the long queue, the sugam darshan ₹300 and Mangla aarti (3–4 AM) ₹500 tickets are booked on the temple trust's website or app. Mobiles and pens are not allowed inside — free lockers are provided for them.

Booking: shrikashivishwanath.org or the ‘Shri Kashi Vishwanath’ app. Counters: Helpdesk, YSK-1, YSK-2, YSK-3, Mandir Chowk, the temple premises and Dashashwamedh Ghat. Aarti money is treated as a donation, so it cannot be cancelled and the date cannot be changed. Sugam darshan may be closed on Sawan Mondays and big festivals.

Important things before you go

  • Nothing like mobiles, smartwatches or pens is allowed inside. Free lockers are at Mandir Chowk, YSK-1, 2, 3 and the Helpdesk.
  • There is no fixed dress code, but go in simple, decent clothes.
  • Entry from the Ganga ghat side is through gate no. 1; the Helpdesk is near gate no. 4.
  • Helpline: 6393131608 (call / WhatsApp), email support@shrikashivishwanath.org.
  • Beware of fake websites: many look-alike websites and WhatsApp numbers sell ‘VIP darshan’ or tickets in this temple's name. Book only through the official website or app given below, and never pay an agent in advance.

How to reach

  • Rail: the temple is about 5 km from Varanasi Junction (Cantt).
  • Air: Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (Babatpur), about 25 km.
  • By boat: get off at Dashashwamedh or Lalita Ghat and enter the Dham directly through the Ganga Dwar.

When is it less crowded

Mondays, Sawan, Mahashivratri and Dev Deepawali are the most crowded. Weekday afternoons are the easiest. Do also see the evening Ganga aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat.

Information checked: October 26, 2026. Official website: shrikashivishwanath.org. Temple timings, fees and rules keep changing — always check the official website before you travel or pay.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Kashi Vishwanath sugam darshan ticket?

₹300 per person. A shastri takes you near the sanctum without queuing, with prasad and a locker included.

How much is the Mangla aarti ticket and how do I book it?

₹500 per person, on shrikashivishwanath.org or the temple trust's app. Reporting at 2:30 am at gate no. 1.

What time does the Kashi Vishwanath temple open?

At 3 AM with the Mangla aarti; the temple stays open until 11 PM.

Can I take my mobile into the temple?

No. No. Mobiles, pens and gadgets are not allowed — keep them in the free lockers.

Is there a dress code at Kashi Vishwanath?

There is no fixed dress code; decent clothes are advised.

Can an aarti ticket be cancelled?

No. Aarti money is treated as a donation; cancellation or date change is not possible.

What arrangements are there for the elderly and the disabled?

Divyang devotees are not charged and are given darshan without queuing.

Where can I watch Kashi Vishwanath live darshan?

Press ‘start darshan’ at the top of this page — the temple trust's channel plays right here.

What is the Kashi Vishwanath helpline number?

6393131608 (call and WhatsApp).
