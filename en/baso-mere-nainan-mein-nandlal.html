Baso Mere Nainan Men Nandlal — Meerabai — full lyrics and English meaning

'Baso Mere Nainan Men Nandlal' is a bhajan by Meerabai in which she describes the enchanting form of Shri Krishna — his dark face, flute, Vaijayanti garland and anklets — and prays to him to dwell in her eyes.

A composition of Meerabai. All sant bhajans.

Text variants: a few words differ between books and singers; the popular/standard text is given here.

Baso mere nainan men nandlal ।

O Nandlal (Shri Krishna), come and dwell in my eyes.

Mohni murti, sanvri surti, naina bane bisal ॥

Your image enchants the mind; your complexion is dark and your eyes are large and beautiful.

Adhar sudharas murli rajat, ur baijanti mal ।

The flute adorns your nectar-like lips, and the Vaijayanti garland is on your chest.

Chhudra ghantika kati tat sobhit, nupur sabad rasal ॥

A waistband of tiny bells adorns your waist, and the sound of the anklets on your feet is sweet.

Mira prabhu santan sukhadai, bhagatabchhal gopal ॥

Meera's Lord is Gopal, who gives joy to the saints and loves his devotees.

॥ Iti ॥

The paath of Baso Mere Nainan Men Nandlal — Meerabai is complete.
