Chhath Vrat Rules: What to Do and Avoid on All Four Days

The Chhath vrat begins on November 13, 2026 (Nahay Khay) and ends on November 16 (Usha Arghya). After eating the Kharna prasad on the evening of November 14, the devotee starts a waterless fast of about 36 hours, which ends on the morning of November 16 with the arghya to the rising sun. The main rules: complete purity, no garlic, onion or meat, clean vessels for prasad, and the devotee sleeps on the floor.

The four days at a glance

DayDateWhat devotees do
Nahay KhayNovember 13, FridayOne sattvic meal after bathing: pumpkin, rice and chana dal
KharnaNovember 14, SaturdayFast all day, gur kheer and roti as prasad in the evening; the waterless fast starts after this
Sandhya ArghyaNovember 15, SundayWaterless fast; arghya to the setting sun at the ghat
Usha ArghyaNovember 16, MondayArghya to the rising sun, then parana (breaking the fast)

Day 1: Nahay Khay rules

  • Bathe in the morning in a river, a pond or at home, and clean the house and kitchen thoroughly.
  • The devotee cooks or eats only after bathing. The food is sattvic, with no garlic or onion at all.
  • The meal is pumpkin curry, rice and chana dal. Many homes use sendha namak (rock salt) instead of regular salt, and ghee instead of oil.
  • The rest of the family also eats sattvic food on these days.

Day 2: Kharna rules

  • Devotees fast all day and eat the prasad after sunset, following the puja.
  • The prasad is gur kheer, roti and banana. It is considered best to cook it in clean vessels on a clay stove.
  • No salt goes into this prasad.
  • The devotee eats the prasad first, then the family.
  • After the Kharna prasad, not even water is taken. This is where the waterless fast begins.

Day 3: Sandhya Arghya rules

  • Prepare the prasad (thekua, kasar) and the soop and daura with complete purity. Do not let anyone taste the prasad.
  • In the evening the daura is carried on the head to the ghat. The soop is laid out with fruit, sugarcane, thekua and a lamp. Full list: Chhath Puja samagri.
  • Devotees stand in the water and offer arghya to the setting sun. Sunset is at 5:27 pm in Delhi and 5:00 pm in Patna. See the arghya time for your city.
  • The arghya is offered with milk and water. Do not offer arghya from a plastic vessel; use a copper or brass lota and a bamboo soop.

Day 4: Usha Arghya and parana

  • Reach the ghat before sunrise. Sunrise is at 6:44 am in Delhi and 6:07 am in Patna.
  • Offer arghya to the rising sun and pray to Chhathi Maiya for the family's wellbeing.
  • Share the prasad at the ghat. Back home, the devotee breaks the fast with prasad and water. This is called parana.
  • After the long waterless fast, start with light prasad and avoid a heavy meal right away.

What to avoid during the whole vrat

  • Keep garlic, onion, meat, alcohol and tobacco out of the house.
  • Devotees do not sleep on a bed or charpai; they sleep on a mat or blanket on the floor.
  • Do not touch puja items or prasad without bathing or with unwashed hands.
  • Do not eat the prasad fruit before the puja is complete.
  • Do not use a torn soop or daura. Buying a new one each year is considered good.
  • Be mindful about food and drink around the fasting devotee, and keep quarrels and harsh words out of the house.

Take care of your health

A long fast without water is hard on the body. Pregnant women, elderly people, and anyone with diabetes, kidney disease or blood pressure problems should ask a doctor first. If you feel dizzy or very weak, do not hesitate to drink water. Do not stand in cold water for too long, and make sure someone stays with children and elderly people at the ghat.

Information checked: October 27, 2026. These are traditions and beliefs; give priority to your own family's customs.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Chhath vrat 2026 begin?

It begins with Nahay Khay on November 13, 2026 (Friday) and ends with Usha Arghya on November 16 (Monday).

When does the waterless Chhath fast start?

After eating the Kharna prasad, on the evening of November 14. It ends after the arghya on the morning of November 16.

How many hours is the Chhath fast?

The waterless fast lasts about 36 hours, from Kharna to Usha Arghya.

What is eaten on Nahay Khay?

Pumpkin curry, rice and chana dal, without garlic or onion.

What is eaten on Kharna?

Gur kheer, roti and banana. It has no salt, and the devotee eats first.

Why do Chhath devotees sleep on the floor?

It is a rule of restraint and simplicity. During these days devotees give up the bed and sleep on a mat or blanket on the floor.

Can men keep the Chhath vrat?

Yes. Yes. Both women and men keep the Chhath vrat.

How is the Chhath fast broken?

On the morning of November 16, after the arghya to the rising sun, break the fast with prasad and water, then have a light meal.
