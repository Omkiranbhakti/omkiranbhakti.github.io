Tulsi Vivah vidhi: how to do it at home, samagri list

Tulsi Vivah is on Saturday, November 21, 2026. Do the puja during Pradosh Kaal, which in New Delhi runs from 5:25 pm to 7:49 pm. Dress the tulsi pot like a bride with a red chunari and suhag items, make a sugarcane mandap, seat Shaligram ji beside her as the groom, then do the haldi, pheras and kanyadan, and finish with aarti.

Tulsi Vivah samagri

  • A tulsi pot and Shaligram ji (if you have no Shaligram, a small idol or picture of Lord Vishnu or Krishna)
  • Sugarcane (4 for the mandap), two chowkis, red and yellow cloth
  • For tulsi: red chunari, bangles, bindi, sindoor, mehndi, a garland and the other suhag items
  • Yellow cloth and a janeu for Shaligram ji
  • Kalash, mango leaves, and an idol or picture of Ganesh ji
  • Haldi, roli, mauli, akshat, til, supari, paan leaves
  • Ghee lamp, dhoop, camphor, flowers and itr
  • Ber, amla, sugarcane, seasonal fruit, panchmeva and sweets
  • Everything in one place: buy puja samagri

Getting ready for the puja

  • Clean the tulsi pot and decorate it with geru or haldi. Remove any dry leaves.
  • Place a chowki in the courtyard, on the terrace or in the puja room and seat the tulsi on it.
  • Stand sugarcane around the chowki, tie the tops together and decorate with flowers. This is the wedding mandap.
  • Spread a red cloth on the second chowki and seat Shaligram ji on it. Tulsi is usually placed on the right side of Shaligram ji.
  • Pick tulsi leaves for the puja a day earlier or during the day, because leaves are not picked after evening. See: Tulsi rules.

Tulsi Vivah vidhi: step by step

  1. In the evening, bathe, put on clean clothes, light a lamp and take the sankalp for the wedding. The family doing the kanyadan may fast through the day.
  2. Place the kalash and remember Ganesh ji first.
  3. Do tulsi mata's shringar: drape the red chunari and offer bangles, bindi, sindoor and a garland.
  4. Bathe Shaligram ji, dress him in yellow cloth and apply chandan.
  5. Apply haldi soaked in milk to both tulsi and Shaligram ji. Put a little haldi on the mandap's sugarcane too.
  6. The women sing wedding mangal geet. Many homes also recite the Mangalashtak at this point.
  7. Kanyadan: The elders of the house take the sankalp to give tulsi mata to Shaligram ji, and tie tulsi and Shaligram together with mauli (gathbandhan).
  8. Pheras: Lift Shaligram ji with his asan and take 7 parikramas of the tulsi.
  9. Offer bhog of ber, amla, sugarcane, fruit and sweets.
  10. Read or listen to the Tulsi Vivah Ki Katha, then do the Tulsi Mata aarti and Om Jai Jagdish Hare.
  11. Finish with pushpanjali, share the prasad and eat together.

Tulsi ji's mantra

You can chant ‘Om Tulasyai Namah’ during the puja. At the end, this traditional shloka with tulsi's names is recited:

Vrinda Vrindavani Vishvapujita Vishvapavani.
Pushpasara Nandini cha Tulasi Krishnajivani.

Keep these points in mind

  • Do not offer rice to Shaligram ji. Worship him with til, flowers and tulsi leaves. In some homes akshat is offered only in the puja of tulsi mata and the mandap.
  • Do not pick tulsi leaves after evening.
  • Do not keep shoes or slippers near the tulsi.
  • If you have no Shaligram at home, the puja can be done with an idol or picture.
  • A big function is not needed. One pot, one lamp and a sincere heart are enough for a complete puja.

On Ekadashi or on Dwadashi

In most panchangs Tulsi Vivah falls on Kartik Shukla Dwadashi, which in 2026 is November 21. Some families do it on the evening of Dev Uthani Ekadashi, that is November 20. Follow the custom of your own home. Those fasting on Ekadashi should do parana on the morning of November 21.

Frequently asked questions

When is Tulsi Vivah 2026?

On November 21, 2026, Saturday. Dwadashi tithi runs from November 21, 6:32 am to November 22, 4:57 am.

What is the shubh time for Tulsi Vivah?

Pradosh Kaal, meaning the evening, is considered best. In New Delhi it runs from 5:25 pm to 7:49 pm.

How do you do Tulsi Vivah at home?

Dress the tulsi pot like a bride, make a sugarcane mandap, seat Shaligram ji beside her, apply haldi, do the pheras, take the kanyadan sankalp, then do aarti and share prasad.

What items are needed for Tulsi Vivah?

A tulsi pot, Shaligram ji, 4 sugarcane, a red chunari and suhag items, yellow cloth, kalash, haldi, roli, mauli, a lamp, flowers, fruit and sweets.

How many pheras are there in Tulsi Vivah?

Usually Shaligram ji is lifted with his asan and 7 parikramas of the tulsi are taken. Some families have a different custom.

How do you do Tulsi Vivah without a Shaligram?

You can worship with a small idol or picture of Lord Vishnu or Krishna as the groom.

Can rice be offered to Shaligram ji?

By tradition, rice is not offered to Shaligram ji. He is worshipped with til, flowers and tulsi leaves.

What is the benefit of doing Tulsi Vivah?

It is believed to bring punya like that of kanyadan, and happiness and good fortune to the home.
