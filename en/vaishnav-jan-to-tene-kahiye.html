Vaishnav Jan To Tene Kahiye — Narsi Mehta — full lyrics and English meaning

'Vaishnav Jan To Tene Kahiye' is a bhajan of the 15th-century Gujarati saint Narsi Mehta. It describes the qualities of a true devotee — one who understands others' pain, is not proud, speaks the truth and stays away from greed and anger. It was a favourite bhajan of Mahatma Gandhi.

A Gujarati composition of Narsi Mehta. All sant bhajans.

Text variants: a few words differ between books and singers; the popular/standard text is given here.

Vaishnav jan to tene kahiye, je pid parai jane re ।
Par duhkhe upkar kare to ye, man abhiman na ane re ॥

A true Vaishnav (devotee) is one who understands the pain of others; who helps the suffering, yet never lets pride enter the mind.

Sakal lokman sahune vande, ninda na kare keni re ।
Vach kachh man nishchal rakhe, dhan dhan janni teni re ॥

He bows to everyone in the world and speaks ill of none; he keeps his speech, deeds and mind pure and steady — blessed is his mother.

Samadrishti ne trishna tyagi, parastri jene mat re ।
Jihva thaki asatya na bole, pardhan nav jhale hath re ॥

He sees all equally, gives up greed, regards other women as mothers; his tongue does not lie and his hand does not touch others' wealth.

Moh maya vyape nahi jene, drirh vairagya jena manman re ।
Ramnam shun tali re lagi, sakal tirath tena tanman re ॥

One whom attachment and Maya do not grip, who has firm detachment in his mind, whose heart is set on Ram-naam — all the tirthas dwell in his body.

Vanlobhi ne kapatarhit chhe, kam krodh nivarya re ।
Bhane narsaiyo tenun darsan kartan, kul ekoter tarya re ॥

One free of greed and deceit, who has conquered desire and anger — Narsi Mehta says, the darshan of such a person liberates seventy-one generations.

॥ Iti ॥

The paath of Vaishnav Jan To Tene Kahiye — Narsi Mehta is complete.

Frequently asked questions

Who wrote 'Vaishnav Jan To Tene Kahiye'?

Narsi Mehta, the saint-poet of Gujarat, in the 15th century.

What does Vaishnav Jan mean?

A true devotee of Lord Vishnu — according to this bhajan, one who understands others' sorrow and is not proud.
