Bahula Chauth Katha

Bahula Chauth Ki Katha is about Bahula, a cow of Shri Krishna. When a lion stopped her in the forest, Bahula promised to return after feeding her calf — and she really did come back. Shri Krishna had tested her in the form of the lion; pleased with her truthfulness, he blessed her.

Bahula Chauth 2027: August 20, 2027, Friday — see the tithi and puja time

The katha of Bahula the cow

In Braj, among Shri Krishna's cows, there was a cow named Bahula who was dear to everyone. One day, while grazing, she got separated from the herd and wandered far into the forest.

Suddenly a lion appeared before her, ready to eat her. Bahula pleaded, 'My little calf is hungry at home. Let me go and feed him one last time, then I will come back to you myself.'

The lion said, 'What is the guarantee you will come back?' Bahula swore by truth and dharma. The lion let her go.

Bahula went home, fed her calf, loved him, and returned to the forest to keep her word. Seeing her truthfulness, the lion took his real form — it was Shri Krishna himself, testing Bahula.

Shri Krishna said, 'Bahula, I am pleased with your truthfulness. From today you will be worshipped on Bhadrapad Krishna Chaturthi, and the children of mothers who keep this vrat will be happy.'

This is OmKiran Bhakti's retelling of the traditional katha in simple English; details of the katha vary slightly from region to region.

Frequently asked questions

Why is cow's milk not drunk on Bahula Chauth?

This day is dedicated to the cow and calf, so that day the cow's milk is left for the calf.
