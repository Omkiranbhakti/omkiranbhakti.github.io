Ekadashi Mata Ki Aarti — Om Jai Ekadashi, lyrics and meaning

The 'Om Jai Ekadshi Mata' aarti names all the Ekadashis of the year in order, from Utpanna in Margashirsha to Padmini in Adhik Maas.

All Ekadashi dates and parana times for 2026–27

Om jai ekadshi, jai ekadshi, jai ekadshi mata ।
Vishnu puja vrat ko dharan kar, shakti mukti pata ॥

O Ekadashi Mata, victory to you; whoever keeps this vrat of Vishnu puja gains strength and liberation.

Tere nam ginaun devi, bhakti pradan karni ।
Gan gaurav ki deni mata, shastron men varni ॥

O goddess who gives bhakti, I recount your names; the shastras call you the mother who gives honour.

Margashirsh ke krishnapaksh ki utpanna, vishvatarni janmi ।
Shukla paksh men hui mokshada, muktidata ban ai ॥

In Margashirsha Krishna paksha Utpanna Ekadashi, who carries the world across, was born; in Shukla paksha came Mokshada, giver of liberation.

Paush ke krishnapaksh ki, saphla namak hai ।
Shuklapaksh men hoy putrada, anand adhik rahai ॥

The Ekadashi of Paush Krishna paksha is Saphala; in Shukla paksha comes Putrada, which brings great joy.

Nam shattila magh mas men, krishnapaksh avai ।
Shuklapaksh men jaya kahavai, vijay sada pavai ॥

In Magh Krishna paksha comes Shattila; the Shukla paksha one is called Jaya, which always brings victory.

Vijya phagun krishnapaksh men, shukla amlaki ।
Papamochni krishna paksh men, chaitra mahabli ki ॥

Vijaya in Phalgun Krishna paksha and Amalaki in Shukla paksha; in Chaitra Krishna paksha the mighty Papmochani.

Chaitra shukla men nam kamda, dhan dene vali ।
Nam baruthini krishnapaksh men, vaisakh mah vali ॥

Kamada of Chaitra Shukla gives wealth; Varuthini is in Vaishakh Krishna paksha.

Shukla paksh men hoy mohini, apra jyeshth krishnapakshi ।
Nam nirjala sab sukh karni, shuklapaksh rakhi ॥

Mohini in Vaishakh Shukla; Apara in Jyeshtha Krishna; and Nirjala, giver of all happiness, in Jyeshtha Shukla.

Yogini nam ashadh men janon, krishnapaksh karni ।
Devashyani nam kahayo, shuklapaksh dharni ॥

The one in Ashadh Krishna paksha is called Yogini and the one in Ashadh Shukla paksha Devshayani.

Kamika shravan mas men avai, krishnapaksh kahie ।
Shravan shukla hoy pavitra, anand se rahie ॥

Kamika comes in Shravan Krishna paksha and Pavitra (Putrada) in Shravan Shukla.

Aja bhadrapad krishnapaksh ki, parivartini shukla ।
Indra ashvin krishnapaksh men, vrat se bhavasagar nikla ॥

Aja of Bhadrapad Krishna, Parivartini of Bhadrapad Shukla, and Indira of Ashwin Krishna, whose vrat carries one across the ocean of worldly life.

Papankusha hai shukla paksh men, ap haranhari ।
Rama mas kartik men avai, sukhadayak bhari ॥

Papankusha of Ashwin Shukla removes sins; Rama of Kartik Krishna gives great happiness.

Devotthani shuklapaksh ki, dukhanashak maiya ।
Pavan mas men karun vinti, par karo naiya ॥

Dev Uthani Ekadashi of Kartik Shukla ends sorrow; in this holy month we pray — carry our boat across.

Parma krishnapaksh men hoti, jan mangal karni ।
Shukla mas men hoy padmini, dukh daridra harni ॥

Parama of Adhik Maas Krishna paksha brings good, and Padmini of Shukla paksha removes sorrow and poverty.

Jo koi arti ekadshi ki, bhakti sahit gavai ।
Jan gurdita swarg ka vasa, nishchay vah pavai ॥

Gurditta says — whoever sings this aarti of Ekadashi with bhakti surely attains heaven.

॥ Iti ॥

The paath of Ekadashi Mata Ki Aarti is complete.
