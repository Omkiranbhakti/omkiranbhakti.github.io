Chhath Songs: Traditional Folk Songs, Their Meaning and Bhajans

Chhath songs mostly come from the Bhojpuri, Maithili and Magahi folk tradition, and women at home have sung them for generations. The best known are ‘Kaanch Hi Baans Ke Bahangiya’, ‘Kelwa Je Farela Ghavad Se’ and ‘Ug Ho Suraj Dev’. Below are their simple meanings, when they are sung, and our Chhath Puja bhajan video.

Popular traditional Chhath songs

SongWhat it is aboutWhen it is sung
Kaanch Hi Baans Ke BahangiyaCarrying prasad to the ghat in a bamboo bahangi; the load bends the pole, but faith does not tireWhile going to the ghat
Kelwa Je Farela Ghavad SeThe story of a parrot hovering over a bunch of bananas: the wish to keep Chhathi Maiya's prasad from being spoiledWhile making prasad and at the ghat
Ug Ho Suraj DevAt dawn, devotees ask the Sun God to rise because the time for arghya has comeBefore Usha Arghya
Jode Jode SupwaOffering soops and fruit in pairs to Chhathi MaiyaAt Sandhya and Usha Arghya
He Dinanath / Ho DinanathCalling the Sun God ‘lord of the humble’ and asking for his graceAt the ghat, during arghya

What makes these songs special

  • They feel like a conversation with Chhathi Maiya and the Sun God: devotees pray simply for their children, husband and home.
  • Bahangi, soop, daura, sugarcane and banana come up again and again, so the whole puja preparation can be heard in the song itself.
  • Because these are folk songs, the words differ a little from village to village and family to family. There is no single ‘correct’ text.
  • We give only the song names and meanings here, not the full lyrics of any artist's recording.

Chhath songs and Sharda Sinha

‘Bihar Kokila’ Sharda Sinha's voice carried Chhath songs to homes across India and abroad. Her songs such as ‘Kelwa Ke Paat Par’ and ‘Ho Dinanath’ are still played at the ghats. She passed away in November 2024. Listen to her recordings on the official channels of her music labels.

Which song to sing when

  • Nahay Khay and Kharna: Chhathi Maiya's songs are sung while cleaning the house and preparing the prasad.
  • Sandhya Arghya: ‘Kaanch Hi Baans Ke Bahangiya’ while carrying the daura to the ghat.
  • At night: Chhathi Maiya's songs during the kosi ritual and while staying awake at the ghat.
  • Usha Arghya: ‘Ug Ho Suraj Dev’ at dawn. After the arghya you can also do the Surya Dev Aarti.

Information checked: October 27, 2026. These are traditions and beliefs; give priority to your own family's customs.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most famous Chhath song?

‘Kaanch Hi Baans Ke Bahangiya’ and ‘Kelwa Je Farela Ghavad Se’ are the best known.

What is a bahangi?

A bamboo pole with a basket hanging from each end. In Chhath it was used to carry prasad to the ghat.

When is Ug Ho Suraj Dev sung?

At dawn before Usha Arghya, while devotees wait for the sun to rise.

Did Sharda Sinha write the Chhath songs?

Many Chhath songs come from folk tradition. Sharda Sinha made them popular with her voice, and some of her songs were also newly written.

What language are Chhath songs in?

Mostly Bhojpuri, Maithili and Magahi.

Is it necessary to sing Chhath songs?

There is no strict rule, but songs are an important part of the Chhath tradition, and women sing them from the puja preparation all the way to the ghat.
