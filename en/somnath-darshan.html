Somnath darshan guide: timings, aarti and rules

Darshan at Somnath Temple is open from 6 am to 10 pm and entry is free. Aarti is held every day at 7 am, 12 noon and 7 pm. Mobile phones, cameras and smartwatches are not allowed inside the temple; they have to be left in the free cloak room. Book puja, donations and trust rooms only on somnath.org.

Different pages of the trust website give the show time as 7:45 and 8:00; aarti and show timings can change in the monsoon.

Darshan arrangements

  • Darshan is free; no ticket is needed.
  • Puja and donations are done through the Shri Somnath Trust website somnath.org; prasad can also be ordered from the link on the trust website.
  • Wheelchairs and golf carts for elderly and disabled devotees are available at the main gate; there is also a lift inside the temple.
  • The trust's ‘Tirth Darshan’ bus to the nearby holy places leaves the main gate at 8:30 am and 3:30 pm (small fee).
  • Beware of fake websites: many look-alike websites and WhatsApp numbers sell ‘VIP darshan’ or tickets in this temple's name. Book only through the official website or app given below, and never pay an agent in advance.

Important things before you go

  • Mobile and camera: mobile phones, cameras, smartwatches, laptops and tablets are all prohibited. Cloak room lockers are free.
  • Leave footwear at the free shoe house near the cloak room.
  • Clothes: sarees, dhotis, trousers and T-shirts are fine; short or disrespectful clothing is not allowed.
  • Photography and smoking are strictly prohibited in the temple complex.

Where to stay

The Shri Somnath Trust runs guest houses with more than 200 rooms: Sagar Darshan, Lilavati Atithi Bhavan, Maheshwari Samaj Atithi Gruh and a budget dormitory. According to the rates on the trust website, a non-AC room starts at about ₹750 and a dormitory bed at about ₹90 (GST extra). Book rooms online only on somnath.org.

How to reach

  • Rail: Somnath station is about half a km from the temple and Veraval Junction about 7 km.
  • Air: Diu airport about 85–95 km; Rajkot about 200 km.
  • Road: about 400 km from Ahmedabad; direct buses run from the big cities of Gujarat.

When is it less crowded

The biggest crowds come on Sawan Mondays, Mahashivratri and Kartik Purnima. On ordinary days darshan is easy early in the morning or after the noon aarti. To see the evening aarti and the light and sound show together, arrive by 6:30.

Information checked: November 16, 2026. Official website: somnath.org. Temple timings, fees and rules keep changing, so always check the official website before travelling or paying.

Frequently asked questions

What time does Somnath Temple open and close?

It opens at 6 am and closes at 10 pm.

What are the aarti timings at Somnath Temple?

There are three aartis every day: at 7 am, 12 noon and 7 pm.

Is there a ticket for Somnath darshan?

No, darshan is free.

What time is the Somnath light and sound show?

After the aarti, from about 8 to 9 at night; there is no show in the monsoon. Check tickets and the latest timing at the temple or on somnath.org.

Can I take my mobile phone into Somnath Temple?

No. Mobiles, cameras and smartwatches have to be deposited in the free lockers.

Which is the nearest railway station to Somnath?

Somnath station (about half a km) and Veraval Junction (about 7 km).

How do I book trust rooms to stay in Somnath?

Through ‘Guest House Booking’ on the Shri Somnath Trust website somnath.org.

Where can I watch Somnath live darshan?

Press ‘Start darshan’ at the top of this page; the broadcast from the temple's official channel will play right here.
