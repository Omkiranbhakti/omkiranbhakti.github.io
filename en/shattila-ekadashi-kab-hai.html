Significance of Shattila Ekadashi

Where it is celebrated: all over India

On this day til (sesame) is used in six ways: bathing with til, til ubtan, havan with til, drinking til water, eating til, and giving til as daan. Hence the name 'Shat-tila' (six sesames).

The story in short: a Brahmin woman kept many fasts but never donated food. To test her, the Lord came as a beggar, and she gave him a lump of clay. In heaven she got a house, but it was empty. Then, as the heavenly women told her, she kept the fast on Shattila Ekadashi with a donation of sesame, and her house filled with grain and wealth.

Special on this day: donating sesame is considered the most important. In the winter season, sesame laddoos, blankets and warm clothes are donated.

Sometimes the Smarta (householder) and Vaishnav Ekadashi fall on different days; followers of the Vaishnav tradition keep the vrat on the next day.

Shattila Ekadashi puja vidhi

  1. From the night before Dashami, eat sattvik food and avoid rice.
  2. On the morning of Ekadashi, bathe and take the sankalp (vow) of the vrat before Lord Vishnu.
  3. Offer yellow flowers, tulsi leaves, fruit and panchamrit to Vishnu ji, and light a ghee diya.
  4. Do jaap of 'Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya', and read the Vishnu Sahasranama or the Ekadashi katha.
  5. During the day keep a phalahar (fruit-only) or nirjala (waterless) vrat as your health allows, and sing bhajan-kirtan at night.
  6. The next day (Dwadashi), after sunrise and before Dwadashi ends, give daan (charity) and break the vrat (parana).

Frequently asked questions

When is Shattila Ekadashi 2027?

Shattila Ekadashi in 2027 falls on February 2, 2027, Tuesday. It is the Ekadashi of the Krishna paksha of Magh month. Do the parana (break the vrat) on February 3: after 7:08 am and before 1:51 pm.

When is the parana of Shattila Ekadashi?

February 3: after 7:08 am, before 1:51 pm (New Delhi).

What is the significance of Shattila Ekadashi?

The Ekadashi of the six uses of til. On this day til (sesame) is used in six ways: bathing with til, til ubtan, havan with til, drinking til water, eating til, and giving til as daan. Hence the name 'Shat-tila' (six sesames).

Why is sesame donated on Shattila Ekadashi?

The story shows that charity is as necessary as fasting. Of the six uses of sesame on this day, donation is considered the greatest.

What can you eat in the Ekadashi vrat?

Fruit, milk, curd, sabudana, kuttu, singhara, potato, sweet potato and sendha (rock) salt. Rice, wheat, dals, onion-garlic and ordinary salt are not eaten.

When should the Ekadashi parana be done?

The next day, during Dwadashi tithi, after sunrise. Parana is best done once Hari Vasar (the first quarter of Dwadashi) has passed.
