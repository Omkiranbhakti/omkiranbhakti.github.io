When is Chaiti Chhath?

Chaiti Chhath in 2027 falls on April 12, 2027, Monday. It is observed from the morning on the Shashthi tithi of the Shukla paksha of Chaitra month.

The Chhath Puja of Chaitra month, in Bihar, Jharkhand and eastern Uttar Pradesh. The festival is kept on the day this tithi prevails at sunrise.

Why is Chaiti Chhath 2027 on this date?

The tithi rule, in simple words

Shashthi tithi runs from 11 April, 9:40 pm to 12 April, 7:35 pm.

The festival is kept on the day this tithi prevails at sunrise.

On 11 April 2027, the tithi would not yet have begun at sunrise (6:02 am).

On Monday, 12 April 2027, this tithi prevails at sunrise (6:01 am) — so Chaiti Chhath falls on this day.

Significance of Chaiti Chhath

Where it is celebrated: Bihar, Jharkhand and eastern Uttar Pradesh

Chhath comes twice a year: in Kartik and in Chaitra. The Chhath of Chaitra is called Chaiti Chhath. Like the Kartik Chhath, it too has a four-day vrat and puja of Surya Dev and Chhathi Maiya.

Chaiti Chhath puja vidhi

  1. Day one: Nahay-Khay.
  2. Day two: Kharna, with prasad of jaggery kheer in the evening.
  3. Day three: arghya to the setting sun (this is the date given above).
  4. Day four: parana after offering arghya to the rising sun.
