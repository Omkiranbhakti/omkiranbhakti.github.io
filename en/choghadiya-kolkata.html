Frequently asked questions

When is the auspicious choghadiya in Kolkata today?

Today, October 29, 2026, Thursday, sunrise in Kolkata is at 5:39 am and sunset at 5:01 pm. The most auspicious choghadiya of the day: Shubh (5:39 am–7:04 am), Labh (11:20 am–12:45 pm), Amrit (12:45 pm–2:10 pm). Rahu kaal lasts 12:45 pm – 2:10 pm.

What is choghadiya?

The day (sunrise to sunset) and the night (sunset to the next sunrise) are each divided into eight equal parts; each part is one choghadiya. Amrit, Shubh and Labh are auspicious, Char is neutral, and Rog, Kaal and Udveg are considered inauspicious.

Why is the choghadiya different in every city?

Because it is worked out from local sunrise and sunset; sunrise in Delhi and Mumbai differs by about half an hour.
