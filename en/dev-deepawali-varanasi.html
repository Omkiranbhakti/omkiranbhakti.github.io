Dev Deepawali Varanasi 2026: date, ghats, Ganga aarti and boats

Dev Deepawali will be celebrated on the evening of Kartik Purnima, Tuesday, November 24, 2026. Lakhs of diyas are lit after sunset on the 84 ghats of Varanasi, and in 2025 the number was put at about 25 lakh. The main Ganga aarti is at Dashashwamedh Ghat. Watching from the ghat is free, but the crowds are very large, so fix your hotel and boat early and reach the ghat area before 4 pm.

Why Dev Deepawali is celebrated

It is believed that Lord Shiva destroyed Tripurasura on Kartik Purnima, and so came to be called Tripurari. To celebrate this victory the gods came down to the banks of the Ganga at Kashi and lit lamps. That is why it is called the Deepawali of the gods. It comes about 15 days after Deepawali.

What to see in Varanasi

PlaceWhat is special
Dashashwamedh GhatThe main Ganga aarti. This is where the crowd is biggest
Assi GhatA large ghat at the southern end, known for its aarti and diyas
Panchganga GhatDiya decorations and aarti. Dev Deepawali is believed to have started here
Chet Singh GhatLaser and light shows have been held here in past years
Namo GhatIn 2025 the first lamp was lit here, and a laser show was also reported here

Programmes change every year. The details above are from past years, so check the 2026 programme against the local administration's announcement.

Ganga aarti time

On normal days in November, the evening Ganga aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat starts at about 5:45 pm and lasts about 45 minutes. The time can change on Dev Deepawali: in 2025 the special aarti was reported to start at about 5:15. That aarti had 21 archaks and 42 girls representing Riddhi-Siddhi. The aarti is held by Ganga Seva Nidhi. Check their announcement for the confirmed 2026 time.

There is no ticket to sit on the ghat and watch the aarti, so beware of anyone selling ‘aarti tickets’.

Booking a boat

  • A boat is the best way to see the lit-up ghats, but demand for boats is very high on this day.
  • Fix your boat at least a week ahead. Many people also take a shared boat.
  • Fares on this day differ from normal days, so before paying ask clearly about the total fare, the timing and the boarding ghat.
  • Take only a licensed boat and always wear a life jacket. Do not board an overcrowded boat.

Crowds and getting there

  • Hotels fill up early, and many people book months ahead.
  • Vehicles are stopped from going near the ghats several hours before the aarti. In past years vehicles could only go as far as points like Maidagin or Luxa, followed by about 10–25 minutes on foot to Dashashwamedh.
  • For 2026 routes and parking, see the Varanasi police notice.
  • Reach the ghat area before 4 pm. In winter the ghat starts filling by 5 pm even on normal days.
  • Carry few valuables, and keep hold of children and elders.
  • Avoid pushing on the steps and near the water. Fix a meeting point in advance.
  • If you want darshan on the same trip, read the Kashi Vishwanath darshan guide. It is very crowded there on this day too.

If you cannot go to Varanasi

Watch the aarti live on Ganga Seva Nidhi's official YouTube channel: Varanasi Ganga aarti live. At home in the evening, light diyas near the tulsi, at the main door and in the puja room, and do the Ganga aarti or Shiv aarti. Diyas and puja items: Buy puja samagri.

Frequently asked questions

When is Dev Deepawali 2026?

On Tuesday, November 24, 2026, the evening of Kartik Purnima.

Why is Dev Deepawali celebrated?

It is believed that Lord Shiva destroyed Tripurasura on this day and the gods celebrated by lighting lamps at Kashi.

Where to see Dev Deepawali in Varanasi?

Dashashwamedh, Assi and Panchganga ghats are the best known. From a boat you can see many ghats at once.

What time is the Ganga aarti on Dev Deepawali?

On normal days in November, at about 5:45 pm. In 2025 the special Dev Deepawali aarti was reported at about 5:15 pm. Check the organisers' announcement for the 2026 time.

How many diyas are lit on Dev Deepawali?

In 2025, about 25 lakh diyas were reported lit on 84 ghats. The number changes every year.

Is there a ticket to see Dev Deepawali?

Watching from the ghat is free. You pay only for a boat or a similar private service.

When should I book a boat for Dev Deepawali?

As early as possible, at least a week ahead.

Can Dev Deepawali be watched live?

Yes. Yes. The Dashashwamedh aarti comes live on Ganga Seva Nidhi's YouTube channel, and it also shows on our Varanasi Ganga aarti live page.
