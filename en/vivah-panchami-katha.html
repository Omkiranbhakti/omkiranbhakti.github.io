Vivah Panchami katha: the Shri Ram–Sita wedding and puja vidhi

Vivah Panchami is on Monday, December 14, 2026. Margashirsha Shukla Panchami tithi runs from 4:48 pm on December 13 to 7:17 pm on December 14 (New Delhi). It is believed that on this day Bhagwan Shri Ram and Mata Sita were married in Janakpur. On this day worship Ram-Sita at home, read the wedding katha, recite the wedding episode from the Bal Kand of the Ramcharitmanas and do the Ramayan ji ki Aarti.

Vivah Panchami Katha

King Janak of Mithila found a baby girl near the tip of his plough while tilling the earth. He named her Sita and raised her as his daughter. King Janak kept a very heavy bow of Bhagwan Shiva, called ‘Pinak’. It is said that as a child Sita ji playfully lifted that bow and put it somewhere else. Then King Janak vowed that Sita would marry whoever could string this bow.

Kings came from far and wide to the swayamvar, but no one could even move the bow. At that time Maharishi Vishwamitra had reached Janakpur with Ram and Lakshman. Before the swayamvar, Ram and Sita saw each other for the first time in the phulwari (garden). Sita ji prayed to Maa Gauri (Parvati) that she would get Ram as her husband.

When all the kings had failed and a sad King Janak said it seemed the earth had no heroes left, Shri Ram rose at the command of Guru Vishwamitra. He bowed to his guru, lifted the bow, and while he was stringing it the bow broke in the middle. Its sound echoed through all three worlds. Tulsidas ji wrote in the Ramcharitmanas:

Let chadhavat khainchat gadhen, kahun na lakha dekh sabu thadhen.
Tehi chhan Ram madhya dhanu tora, bhare bhuvan dhuni ghor kathora.

Meaning: When Shri Ram lifted the bow, when he strung it and when he drew it, no one standing there could even see. In that very moment Ram broke the bow in the middle, and its harsh sound filled all the worlds.

After this Sita ji placed the jaymala around Shri Ram's neck. Hearing the bow break, Bhagwan Parshuram came in anger, but seeing Shri Ram's calm nature and recognising the form of Bhagwan Vishnu in him, he went back. King Janak sent a message to Ayodhya. King Dasharath came to Janakpur with the wedding procession, and on Margashirsha Shukla Panchami Shri Ram and Sita were married with Vedic rites. In the same mandap Lakshman married Urmila, Bharat married Mandavi and Shatrughna married Shrutakirti.

Some details of the story differ slightly between the Valmiki Ramayan and the Ramcharitmanas; the popular version is given here in simple language.

Puja samagri

  • A picture or idol of Shri Ram and Sita (a Ram Darbar will also do)
  • Yellow and red cloth, a chowki
  • A chunri and suhag items for Mata Sita (bangles, bindi, sindoor, mehndi)
  • Two flower garlands (as the jaymala), yellow flowers, tulsi leaves
  • Ghee lamp, dhoop, roli, akshat, kalava
  • Fruit, sweets, panchamrit
  • A copy of the Ramcharitmanas or Ramayan
  • Everything in one place: buy puja samagri

How to do the puja at home

  1. In the morning bathe, wear clean clothes and take the sankalp for the puja of Shri Ram and Sita.
  2. Place Shri Ram's picture on a yellow cloth and Mata Sita's picture on a red cloth on the chowki. First meditate on Ganesh ji.
  3. Offer yellow clothes or yellow flowers to Shri Ram, and a chunri and suhag items to Mata Sita.
  4. Apply roli-akshat to both and offer flower garlands. In many homes the two garlands are exchanged between them as the jaymala ritual, and the two are tied together with kalava (gathbandhan).
  5. Light the ghee lamp and read the wedding katha above, or recite the wedding episode from the Bal Kand of the Ramcharitmanas.
  6. Chant ‘Om Janakivallabhaya Namah’ or ‘Shri Ram Jai Ram Jai Jai Ram’.
  7. Do the Ramayan ji ki Aarti, offer bhog and share the prasad.

In the evening you can also read Ram Chalisa or Hanuman Chalisa.

Vivah Panchami in Janakpur and Ayodhya

Janakpur Dham in Nepal is considered Mata Sita's maternal home. At its Janaki Mandir, Vivah Panchami is celebrated over several days: a symbolic wedding procession arrives from Ayodhya, there are tableaux and rituals of the Ram-Sita wedding, and large numbers of devotees come from India and Nepal. Temples in Ayodhya also celebrate the Ram-Sita wedding on this day. Ayodhya darshan information: Ram Mandir darshan.

Do people get married on Vivah Panchami?

Although this day marks the Ram-Sita wedding, in Mithila and some other regions people avoid marrying off their daughters on this day. The folk belief is that after her marriage Mata Sita had to face exile and great suffering, so people do not want their daughters to have such a life. On the other hand, in many places it is considered auspicious and weddings do take place. Have your wedding date fixed only by your pandit ji.

Information checked: October 27, 2026. These are traditions and beliefs; give priority to your own family's customs.

Frequently asked questions

When is Vivah Panchami 2026?

On December 14, 2026, Monday. Panchami tithi is from December 13 4:48 pm to December 14 7:17 pm (New Delhi).

Why is Vivah Panchami celebrated?

It is believed that Bhagwan Shri Ram and Mata Sita were married in Janakpur on Margashirsha Shukla Panchami. This day is celebrated in its memory.

Where did the Ram-Sita wedding take place?

In Janakpur, the capital of Mithila, which is in Nepal today. The Janaki Mandir stands there.

Which bow did Shri Ram break?

The bow of Bhagwan Shiva, called Pinak. King Janak had vowed that Sita would marry whoever strung it.

Is it auspicious to marry on Vivah Panchami?

In Mithila and some regions people do not marry off their daughters on this day, because Mata Sita had to suffer a lot after her marriage. In many places weddings do take place. Consult your pandit ji.

What should be recited on Vivah Panchami?

The Sita swayamvar and wedding episode from the Bal Kand of the Ramcharitmanas, Ram Chalisa and the Ramayan ji ki Aarti.

What is the puja time on Vivah Panchami?

On December 14, 2026, Panchami tithi lasts from morning until 7:17 pm. You can do the puja any time in between.

When is Vivah Panchami in 2027?

On December 3, 2027, Friday.
