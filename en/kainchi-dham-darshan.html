Kainchi Dham Darshan Guide: route, shuttle and rules

Kainchi Dham is on the Bhowali–Almora road, about 17 km from Nainital and about 9 km from Bhowali. Darshan is free. On weekends and at the June 15 mela, private vehicles are stopped at the Bhowali and Bhimtal parkings and you go on to the Dham by shuttle. Kainchi Dham has no official live broadcast at present.

Is there live darshan of Kainchi Dham?

As of October 26, 2026, we found no official live broadcast by the Shri Kainchi Hanuman Mandir Trust. Most channels on YouTube named ‘Kainchi Dham live’ are private ones that replay old videos. Photography and filming inside the temple are generally not allowed. As soon as an official live stream starts, we will add it to our live darshan page.

How to reach

  • Rail: Kathgodam station, about 38 km; from there by taxi or bus via Bhowali.
  • Air: Pantnagar airport, about 70 km.
  • Road: about 17 km from Nainital and about 9 km from Bhowali.

Weekend parking and shuttle

  • Outside vehicles coming via Bhimtal stop at the Vikas Bhawan parking in Bhimtal; from there a shuttle goes to Bhowali and Kainchi Dham.
  • Vehicles coming from Nainital and Gethia stop at the Bhowali Sanatorium parking; a shuttle runs from there.
  • On weekends this arrangement applies from 5 AM to 8 PM (as per the police plan of October 2026); always check the latest notice from the Nainital police.

June 15 — Pratishtha Divas mela

Kainchi Dham was consecrated on June 15, so every year a big mela and bhandara is held on this day, when lakhs of devotees come for the malpua prasad. For several days around the mela, vehicles cannot drive up to the Dham — the whole journey is by shuttle.

Important things before you go

  • Darshan is free; do not pay anyone for ‘VIP darshan’.
  • The ashram's rules are strict — keep silence and do not take photos or videos inside.
  • The main temple at Kainchi Dham is of Hanuman ji; reciting the Hanuman Chalisa here is a tradition.
  • Return before dark — driving on the hill road at night is difficult.

Information checked: October 26, 2026. Temple timings, fees and rules keep changing — always check the official website before you travel or pay.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kainchi Dham have live darshan?

As of October 26, 2026, there is no official live broadcast by the temple trust. Most channels named ‘live’ are private.

How far is Kainchi Dham from Nainital?

About 17 km; about 9 km from Bhowali.

Which is the nearest railway station to Kainchi Dham?

Kathgodam, about 38 km.

Where does the Kainchi Dham shuttle start?

On weekends and at the mela, from the Bhowali Sanatorium parking and the Vikas Bhawan parking in Bhimtal.

When is the Kainchi Dham mela?

Every year on June 15, the Dham's Pratishtha Divas (consecration day).

Is there a ticket for darshan at Kainchi Dham?

No, darshan is free.
