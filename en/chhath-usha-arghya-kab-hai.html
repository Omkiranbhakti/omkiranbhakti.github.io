When is Chhath Usha Arghya?

Chhath Usha Arghya in 2026 falls on November 16, 2026, Monday. It is observed from the morning on the Saptami tithi of the Shukla paksha of Kartik month.

Parana of the Chhath vrat after arghya to the rising sun. The festival is kept on the day this tithi prevails at sunrise.

Why is Chhath Usha Arghya 2026 on this date?

The tithi rule, in simple words

Saptami tithi runs from 16 November, 2:02 am to 17 November, 4:20 am.

The festival is kept on the day this tithi prevails at sunrise.

On Monday, 16 November 2026, this tithi prevails at sunrise (6:46 am) — so Chhath Usha Arghya falls on this day.

On 17 November 2026, the tithi would already have ended by sunrise (6:47 am).
