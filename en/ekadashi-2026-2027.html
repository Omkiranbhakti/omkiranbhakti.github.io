Frequently asked questions

When is the next Ekadashi?

The next Ekadashi is Rama Ekadashi, on November 5, 2026, Thursday.

When should the Ekadashi vrat be broken (parana)?

The parana is done the next day (on Dwadashi) after sunrise and before Dwadashi tithi ends. The parana time of every Ekadashi is given in the table.

How many Ekadashis are there in a year?

Two every month, so 24 Ekadashis in a year. In a year with an adhik maas there are 26.
