Kaal Bhairav Jayanti Puja Vidhi: katha, mantra and rules

Kaal Bhairav Jayanti falls on December 1, 2026, Tuesday. Ashtami tithi is from December 1, 12:12 am to 11:14 pm (New Delhi). In the evening or at night, light a mustard oil lamp before Bhairav Baba, offer black sesame, urad, coconut and imarti or jalebi as bhog, chant 'Om Kalabhairavaya Namah' and read Bhairav Chalisa and the aarti. There is also a tradition of feeding roti to a black dog on this day.

The main puja of Bhairav Jayanti is at midnight (nishith kaal), but in 2026 the Ashtami tithi ends at 11:14 pm, before nishith kaal. So the day on which the tithi begins (December 1) has been taken. In such a case many people do the evening or night puja while Ashtami lasts, that is, before 11:14 pm. Confirm the time with your pandit ji as per your tradition.

Katha of Kaal Bhairav

The Shiva Purana tells that once Brahma ji and Vishnu ji argued over who was the greatest. At that moment a radiant pillar of light appeared and Lord Shiva revealed his supreme form. Even then Brahma ji insulted Shiva ji with his fifth head.

Then from Lord Shiva's anger a fierce form appeared, which was called Kaal Bhairav. Bhairav cut off that fifth head of Brahma ji with his fingernail. After this the sin of Brahmahatya followed him, and Brahma ji's skull (kapal) stuck to his hand.

Carrying the skull in his hand, Bhairav wandered through the three worlds. When he reached Kashi, the skull fell from his hand and he was freed from the sin. The place where the skull fell came to be called the 'Kapal Mochan' tirth. Lord Shiva made Bhairav the kotwal (guardian) of Kashi. Even today it is believed that one should have darshan at the Kaal Bhairav temple before or after Kashi Vishwanath darshan.

It is believed that this happened on Margashirsha Krishna Ashtami, so this day is celebrated as Kaal Bhairav Jayanti or Kalashtami. The dog is considered Bhairav Baba's vahan (vehicle).

Puja samagri

  • Picture or idol of Bhairav Baba or Lord Shiva
  • Mustard oil lamp (clay), cotton wick
  • Black sesame, whole urad, sindoor, flowers (marigold or blue flowers)
  • Coconut, imarti or jalebi, paan-supari
  • Incense, akshat, a lota of water
  • Roti for a black dog (with mustard oil or jaggery)
  • Everything in one place: buy puja samagri

Puja vidhi: step by step

  1. Bathe in the morning, meditate on Lord Shiva and Bhairav Baba and take the vrat or puja sankalp. If you like, visit a nearby Shiva or Bhairav temple.
  2. In the evening clean the puja place and place Bhairav Baba's picture on the chowki. First meditate on Ganesh ji.
  3. Light the mustard oil lamp. Offer sindoor, flowers, black sesame and urad to Bhairav Baba.
  4. Offer coconut, imarti or jalebi and paan as bhog.
  5. Chant the mantra 'Om Kalabhairavaya Namah' 108 times.
  6. Read the katha above, then read the Kalabhairava Ashtakam given below or Bhairav Chalisa.
  7. Do Bhairav Baba's aarti and distribute prasad.
  8. Feed roti to a black dog. Donate a blanket, grain or black sesame to someone in need.

Those keeping the vrat eat fruit during the day and break the fast after the puja or the next day.

Mantra and Kalabhairava Ashtakam

Mool mantra: Om Kalabhairavaya Namah.

The first shloka of the Kalabhairava Ashtakam composed by Adi Shankaracharya:

Devaraja-sevyamana-pavananghri-pankajam
Vyala-yajnasutram-indushekharam kripakaram.
Naradadi-yogivrinda-vanditam digambaram
Kashikapuradhinatha-kalabhairavam bhaje.

Meaning: I worship Kaal Bhairav, the lord of the city of Kashi, whose holy lotus feet are served by Indra, king of the gods, who wears a snake as his sacred thread, who has the moon on his head, who is an ocean of mercy, who is praised by yogis like Narada, and who is digambara (clad in the directions).

Rules for this day

  • Do not trouble or hit any dog or animal.
  • Avoid lying, quarrelling and insulting anyone.
  • Keep away from meat, alcohol and tamasic food at home.
  • Light the lamp with mustard oil and keep it away from wind and curtains.
  • Do not bring fear or totkas into Bhairav puja. Bhairav Baba is regarded as the protector of devotees.

The Kaal Bhairav temple in Ujjain has its own bhog tradition, but at home offer only sattvic bhog. Ujjain travel information: Mahakal darshan booking.

Frequently asked questions

When is Kaal Bhairav Jayanti 2026?

On December 1, 2026, Tuesday. Ashtami tithi is from December 1, 12:12 am to 11:14 pm (New Delhi).

At what time should the puja be done on Kaal Bhairav Jayanti?

The main puja is done at night. In 2026 the Ashtami ends at 11:14 pm, so many people do the evening or night puja before that.

What bhog is offered to Kaal Bhairav?

Imarti or jalebi, coconut, paan, black sesame and urad.

What is the mantra of Kaal Bhairav?

'Om Kalabhairavaya Namah'. Besides this, Adi Shankaracharya's Kalabhairava Ashtakam is also recited.

Why is a black dog fed on Kaal Bhairav Jayanti?

The dog is considered Bhairav Baba's vahan, so there is a tradition of feeding it roti on this day.

Why is Kaal Bhairav called the kotwal of Kashi?

According to the katha, after Bhairav was freed from the sin of Brahmahatya in Kashi, Lord Shiva made him the guardian (kotwal) of Kashi.

What is the difference between Kaal Bhairav Jayanti and Kalashtami?

The Krishna Ashtami of every month is called Kalashtami. Bhairav is believed to have appeared on Margashirsha Krishna Ashtami, so that day is Kaal Bhairav Jayanti.

When is Kaal Bhairav Jayanti in 2027?

On November 20, 2027, Saturday.
