Om Jai Lakshmi Mata — Lyrics in English with Meaning

'Om Jai Lakshmi Mata' is the aarti of Goddess Lakshmi, sung at the Diwali Lakshmi puja, on Dhanteras and on Fridays. The aarti is given below in English letters and Devanagari, with the meaning of each verse in English.

Sing it on Diwali night after Lakshmi puja, on Dhanteras and every Friday evening.

Om jai lakshmi mata, maiya jai lakshmi mata ।
Tumko nishdin sevat, hari vishnu vidhata ॥

ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता, मैया जय लक्ष्मी माता। तुमको निशदिन सेवत, हरि विष्णु विधाता॥

Victory to you, Mother Lakshmi! Vishnu and Brahma serve you day and night.

Uma rama brahmani, tum hi jag-mata ।
Surya-chandrama dhyavat, narad rishi gata ॥

उमा रमा ब्रह्माणी, तुम ही जग-माता। सूर्य-चन्द्रमा ध्यावत, नारद ऋषि गाता॥

You are Uma, Rama and Brahmani — the mother of the world; the sun and moon meditate on you and the sage Narad sings your stuti.

Durga rup niranjni, sukh sampatti data ।
Jo koi tumko dhyata, riddhi-siddhi dhan pata ॥

दुर्गा रूप निरंजनी, सुख सम्पत्ति दाता। जो कोई तुमको ध्याता, ऋद्धि-सिद्धि धन पाता॥

You are the pure form of Durga, giver of happiness and wealth; whoever meditates on you gains prosperity, success and riches.

Tum patal-nivasini, tum hi shubhdata ।
Karm-prabhav-prakashini, bhavnidhi ki trata ॥

तुम पाताल-निवासिनि, तुम ही शुभदाता। कर्म-प्रभाव-प्रकाशिनी, भवनिधि की त्राता॥

You dwell even in the netherworld, you grant all good, you reveal the fruit of our actions and carry us across the ocean of life.

Jis ghar men tum rahtin, sab sadgun ata ।
Sab sambhav ho jata, man nahin ghabrata ॥

जिस घर में तुम रहतीं, सब सद्गुण आता। सब सम्भव हो जाता, मन नहीं घबराता॥

In the home where you live every virtue arrives; everything becomes possible and the mind is free of worry.

Tum bin yagya na hote, vastra na koi pata ।
Khan-pan ka vaibhav, sab tumse ata ॥

तुम बिन यज्ञ न होते, वस्त्र न कोई पाता। खान-पान का वैभव, सब तुमसे आता॥

Without you no sacred ritual could happen and no one would have clothes; all the abundance of food and drink comes from you.

Shubh-gun mandir sundar, kshiroddhi-jata ।
Ratna chaturdash tum bin, koi nahin pata ॥

शुभ-गुण मन्दिर सुन्दर, क्षीरोदधि-जाता। रत्न चतुर्दश तुम बिन, कोई नहीं पाता॥

You are a beautiful temple of good qualities, born from the ocean of milk; without you no one obtains the fourteen jewels.

Mahalakshmiji ki arti, jo koi jan gata ।
Ur anand samata, pap utar jata ॥

महालक्ष्मीजी की आरती, जो कोई जन गाता। उर आनन्द समाता, पाप उतर जाता॥

Whoever sings this aarti of Mahalakshmi is filled with joy in the heart, and their sins fall away.

The text is traditional and wording varies slightly by region. The English meanings are written by OmKiran Bhakti. Read this page in Hindi.
