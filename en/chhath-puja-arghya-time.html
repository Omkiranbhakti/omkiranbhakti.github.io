Chhath Puja 2026: arghya time in your city

In Chhath Puja 2026, the sandhya arghya is on November 15, 2026, Sunday and the usha arghya on November 16, 2026, Monday. In Patna, arghya to the setting sun is at 5:00 pm and to the rising sun at 6:07 am; in Delhi at 5:27 pm and 6:44 am.

Frequently asked questions

What is the arghya time in Patna on Chhath Puja 2026?

Sandhya arghya (November 15, 2026, Sunday) at sunset 5:00 pm; usha arghya (November 16, 2026, Monday) at sunrise 6:07 am.

What is the arghya time in Delhi on Chhath Puja 2026?

Sandhya arghya at sunset 5:27 pm; usha arghya at sunrise 6:44 am.

At what time should arghya be offered in Chhath?

The sandhya arghya is offered to the setting sun just before sunset, and the usha arghya to the rising sun at sunrise. It is good to reach the ghat 15–20 minutes early.
