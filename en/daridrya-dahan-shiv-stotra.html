Daridrya Dahan Shiv Stotra — full paath and English meaning

Daridrya Dahan Shiv Stotra is a composition of Maharishi Vasishtha. Every shloka ends with 'Daridrya Duhkha Dahanaya Namah Shivaya' — I bow to Shiva who burns away the sorrow of poverty.

On Monday and on Pradosh, at all three sandhyas — morning, midday and evening — along with Shiv Chalisa.

1

Vishveshvaray narakarnavatarnay
Karnamritay shashishekharadharnay ।
Karpurakantidhavlay jatadhray
Daridryaduhkhadahnay namah shivay ॥

Lord of the universe, who carries us across the ocean of hell, like nectar to the ears, who wears the moon on his head, bright as camphor, with matted locks — I bow to Shiva who burns away the sorrow of poverty.

2

Gauripriyay rajanishakaladhray
Kalantakay bhujagadhipakankanay ।
Gangadhray gajarajavimardanay
Daridryaduhkhadahnay namah shivay ॥

Beloved of Gauri, wearer of the crescent moon, who ends even death, who wears the king of serpents as a bracelet, bearer of the Ganga, slayer of Gajasura — I bow to Shiva who burns away the sorrow of poverty.

3

Bhaktipriyay bhavarogabhayaphay
Ugray durgabhavasagaratarnay ।
Jyotirmayay gunanamasunrityakay
Daridryaduhkhadahnay namah shivay ॥

Lover of devotion, remover of the disease and fear of worldly life, of fierce form, who carries us across the hard ocean of existence, full of light, beautiful dancer — I bow to Shiva who burns away the sorrow of poverty.

4

Charmambaray shavabhasmavilepnay
Bhalekshanay manikundalamanditay ।
Manjirapadayuglay jatadhray
Daridryaduhkhadahnay namah shivay ॥

Who wears a hide, smears himself with pyre-ash, has a third eye on his forehead, is adorned with jewelled earrings and wears anklets on both feet, with matted locks — I bow to Shiva who burns away the sorrow of poverty.

5

Panchannay phanirajavibhushnay
Hemanshukay bhuvanatrayamanditay ।
Anandabhumivarday tamomyay
Daridryaduhkhadahnay namah shivay ॥

Five-faced, adorned with the king of serpents, with golden garments, who adorns the three worlds, giver of boons in the land of bliss (Kashi), lord of tamas — I bow to Shiva who burns away the sorrow of poverty.

6

Bhanupriyay bhavasagaratarnay
Kalantakay kamalasanapujitay ।
Netratrayay shubhalakshanalakshitay
Daridryaduhkhadahnay namah shivay ॥

Beloved of Surya, who carries us across the ocean of existence, ender of death, worshipped by Brahma, three-eyed, with auspicious marks — I bow to Shiva who burns away the sorrow of poverty.

7

Ramapriyay raghunathavarapraday
Nagapriyay narakarnavatarnay ।
Punyeshu punyabharitay surarchitay
Daridryaduhkhadahnay namah shivay ॥

Beloved of Ram, giver of boons to Raghunath, beloved of serpents, who carries us across the ocean of hell, full of merit, worshipped by the gods — I bow to Shiva who burns away the sorrow of poverty.

8

Mukteshvaray phalday ganeshvaray
Gitapriyay vrishabheshvaravahnay ।
Matangacharmavasnay maheshvaray
Daridryaduhkhadahnay namah shivay ॥

Lord of liberation, giver of fruits, lord of the ganas, lover of music, rider of Nandi, Maheshwar who wears an elephant hide — I bow to Shiva who burns away the sorrow of poverty.

9

Vasishthen kritan stotran sarvaroganivaranam ।
Sarvasampatkaran shighran putrapautradivardhanam ।
Trisandhyan yah pathennityan sa hi swargamavapnuyat ॥

This stotra composed by Rishi Vasishtha removes all diseases, soon gives every kind of wealth and increases the family line; whoever reads it daily at the three sandhyas attains heaven.

॥ Iti ॥

The paath of Daridrya Dahan Shiv Stotra is complete.

Frequently asked questions

Who wrote the Daridrya Dahan Stotra?

Maharishi Vasishtha; the last shloka says so and gives the fruit of reciting it at the three sandhyas.
