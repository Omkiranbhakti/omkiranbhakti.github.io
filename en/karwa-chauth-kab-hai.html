When is Karwa Chauth?

Karwa Chauth in 2026 falls on October 29, 2026, Thursday. It is observed at night, at moonrise, on the Chaturthi tithi of the Krishna paksha of Kartik month.

What is the Karwa Chauth 2026 puja muhurat?

Thursday, 29 October 2026 — 5:38 pm to 6:54 pm (New Delhi). Most panchangs give about an hour and a quarter after sunset for the Karwa Mata puja. Moon: Delhi 8:12 pm, Mumbai 8:56 pm (approx.). Moonrise in your city

Is there Bhadra on Karwa Chauth 2026?

No. Bhadra runs from 28 October, 2:37 pm to 29 October, 1:07 am — it ends before sunrise on the fast day. There is no Bhadra during the evening puja or at moonrise.

Why is Karwa Chauth 2026 on this date?

The tithi rule, in simple words

Chaturthi tithi runs from 29 October, 1:07 am to 29 October, 10:11 pm.

The moon matters here, so the day is chosen on whose night this tithi prevails at moonrise.

On Thursday, 29 October 2026, this tithi prevails at moonrise (night, 8:30 pm) — so Karwa Chauth falls on this day.

Significance of Karwa Chauth

🪔 Karwa Mata Ki Aarti — full lyrics and meaning

Where it is celebrated: North India: Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh

On Karwa Chauth, married women keep a nirjala vrat from sunrise to moonrise for the long life and good health of their husbands. In the evening Karwa Mata, Shiv-Parvati and Ganesh ji are worshipped, and the vrat is broken after offering arghya to the moon.

A 'karwa' is a clay pot with a spout, used to offer arghya to the moon. In many places unmarried girls now also keep this vrat for a good life partner.

Karwa Chauth puja vidhi

  1. Before sunrise, eat the sargi given by your mother-in-law (a Punjab-Haryana tradition).
  2. Keep the nirjala vrat through the day and do solah shringar.
  3. In the evening, worship Karwa Mata, Shiv-Parvati, Ganesh and Kartikeya, and listen to the Karwa Chauth katha.
  4. Do the thali-pherna ritual (seven times in many places).
  5. When the moon rises, look at the moon and then your husband through a chhalni, and offer arghya from the karwa.
  6. Break the vrat by drinking water and eating a sweet from your husband's hand.

Frequently asked questions

When is Karwa Chauth 2026?

Karwa Chauth in 2026 falls on October 29, 2026, Thursday. It is observed at night, at moonrise, on the Chaturthi tithi of the Krishna paksha of Kartik month.

On which tithi is Karwa Chauth celebrated?

Kartik month, Krishna paksha, Chaturthi. The moon matters here, so the day is chosen on whose night this tithi prevails at moonrise.

What is the significance of Karwa Chauth?

A nirjala vrat of married women, broken after offering arghya to the moon.

When is sargi eaten on Karwa Chauth?

Before sunrise, usually between 4 and 5 in the morning. Sargi has dry fruits, fruit, sweets and feni.

How is the Karwa Chauth vrat broken?

By offering arghya to the moon from the karwa, looking at the moon and then the husband through a chhalni, and drinking water from the husband's hand.
