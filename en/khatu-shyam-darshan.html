Khatu Shyam Darshan Guide: aarti timings, route and mela

Darshan of Khatu Shyam ji is completely free — there is no VIP or online ticket. In winter the mangla aarti is at 5:30 am and the shayan aarti at 9 PM. The nearest railway station is Ringas (about 17 km). The biggest Phalgun (Lakkhi) mela is held on Phalgun Shukla Ekadashi — on March 18, 2027.

Aarti timings (winter)

AartiTime
Mangla aarti5:30 am
Shringar aarti8:00 am
Bhog aarti12:30 pm
Sandhya (evening) aartiEvening, about 6:15 – 6:30
Shayan (night) aarti9:00 pm

These are the winter timings from Kartik. In summer the temple committee changes the timings, so check the temple's notice before you go.

Darshan arrangements

  • Darshan is free and everyone uses the same queue. The temple has no VIP ticket or online darshan pass.
  • On crowded days there are long zig-zag queues; carry water and look after children and the elderly.
  • The tradition is to offer Baba raw milk, roses, ittar (perfume) and peacock feathers.
  • Many devotees bathe in Shyam Kund before darshan.
  • Beware of fake websites: many look-alike websites and WhatsApp numbers sell ‘VIP darshan’ or tickets in this temple's name. Book only through the official website or app given below, and never pay an agent in advance.

Nishan yatra

Many devotees walk about 17 km from Ringas to Khatu and offer Baba a saffron nishan (flag). The nishan is taken around the temple in parikrama and then offered. This yatra is at its biggest during the Phalgun mela.

How to reach

  • Rail: Ringas Junction is the nearest (about 17 km); buses, jeeps and taxis run from there. A new station about 11 km from the temple was announced in July 2026.
  • Air: Jaipur airport, about 80 km.
  • Road: direct buses run from Jaipur, Delhi and Sikar.

When is it most crowded

Every month's Shukla Ekadashi, Dwadashi, Sundays and the Phalgun mela are the most crowded days. New Year is also very crowded. For a relaxed darshan, choose an ordinary weekday, early morning or afternoon.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a ticket for darshan at the Khatu Shyam temple?

No. Darshan is free and there is no VIP ticket. Beware of people selling ‘VIP darshan’ online.

What time is the Khatu Shyam mangla aarti?

In winter, at 5:30 am. The timings change in summer.

What time does the Khatu Shyam temple close at night?

In winter the shayan aarti is at 9 PM, and the doors close after it.

Which is the nearest railway station to Khatu Shyam?

Ringas Junction, about 17 km from the temple.

How far is Khatu Shyam from Jaipur?

About 80 km; around 2 hours by road.

When is the Khatu Shyam Phalgun mela 2027?

The main day of the mela is Phalgun Shukla Ekadashi, which falls on March 18, 2027 (Thursday).

What is the nishan yatra?

The tradition of walking from Ringas to Khatu and offering Baba a saffron flag (nishan).

Which is the best day to visit Khatu Shyam?

To avoid crowds, any weekday other than Ekadashi, Sundays and mela days.
