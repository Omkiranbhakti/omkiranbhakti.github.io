Dwadash Jyotirlinga Stotra — Saurashtre Somnatham, 12 Jyotirlingas

In the shloka 'Saurashtre Somnatham Cha', the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva — Somnath, Mallikarjun, Mahakal, Omkareshwar, Vaidyanath, Bhimashankar, Rameshwaram, Nageshwar, Kashi Vishwanath, Trimbakeshwar, Kedarnath and Grishneshwar — come in order.

Reciting it morning and evening is considered equal to the darshan of all twelve Jyotirlingas.

1

Saurashtre somnath cha shrishaile mallikarjunam ।
Ujjayinyan mahakalamonkaramamaleshvaram ॥

Somnath in Saurashtra (Gujarat), Mallikarjun at Shrishail (Andhra Pradesh), Mahakal in Ujjain (Madhya Pradesh), and Mamaleshwar at Omkareshwar.

2

Paralyan vaidyanathan cha dakinyan bhimashankaram ।
Setubandhe tu rameshan nageshan darukavne ॥

Vaidyanath at Parli (Maharashtra/Deoghar), Bhimashankar at Dakini (Maharashtra), Rameshwaram at Setubandh, and Nageshwar in the Daruka forest.

3

Varanasyan tu vishveshan tryambakan gautamitte ।
Himalye tu kedaran ghushmeshan cha shivalye ॥

Kashi Vishwanath in Varanasi, Trimbakeshwar on the bank of the Godavari (Gautami), Kedarnath in the Himalayas, and Grishneshwar at Shivalaya (Verul).

Doha

Etani jyotirlingani sayan pratah pathennarah ।
Saptajanmakritan papan smarnen vinashyati ॥

Whoever recites these twelve Jyotirlingas morning and evening has the sins of seven births destroyed by mere remembrance.

॥ Iti ॥

The paath of the Dwadash Jyotirlinga Stotra is complete.
