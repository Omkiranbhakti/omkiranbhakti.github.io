Frequently asked questions

How do I book the Mahakal bhasma aarti?

On the temple's official website shrimahakaleshwar.mp.gov.in, ₹200 per person — up to one month ahead, plus tatkal booking at 8 AM the day before. Full details

How do I book the Vaishno Devi helicopter?

Only on the Shrine Board's website online.maavaishnodevi.org; ₹2,320 one way (reported to become about ₹2,700 from November 1, 2026). Full details

When will the Kedarnath doors close?

On November 11, 2026 (Bhai Dooj); Badrinath's on November 16, 2026. Full details

How much is Kashi Vishwanath sugam darshan?

₹300 per person; Mangla aarti ₹500. Full details

What are the darshan timings at the Ram Mandir?

General darshan from 7 AM to about 9:15 PM; all passes free. Full details

Where is the Tirupati ₹300 ticket booked?

Only on TTD's website ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in or the TTD app, about three months ahead. Full details

Is there VIP darshan at Khatu Shyam?

No, darshan is free and there is one queue for everyone. Full details

Does Kainchi Dham have live darshan?

There is no official live broadcast at present. Full details

How do I avoid websites selling VIP darshan in a temple's name?

Book only on the temple's or the government's official website (listed in the table above), and never send money to an agent on WhatsApp.
