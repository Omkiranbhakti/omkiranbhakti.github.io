Kartik Purnima 2026: snan muhurat, daan and deepdaan

Kartik Purnima is on Tuesday, November 24, 2026. Bathing before sunrise is considered best: in New Delhi, Brahma muhurat is roughly 5:14 am to 6:02 and sunrise is at 6:50 am. After the bath, donate grain, jaggery, sesame, warm clothes or blankets, and diyas. In the evening, after sunset (about 5:24), offer deepdaan at the riverbank, near the tulsi, at the main door and in the temple. Purnima tithi lasts until 8:24 pm.

Brahma muhurat is the period roughly 96 to 48 minutes before sunrise. Sunrise differs by a few minutes in other cities, so check your city's sunrise in Today's Panchang. Snan and daan can also be done through the day after sunrise.

Importance of snan on Kartik Purnima

Throughout the month of Kartik, people follow the practice of a morning bath, deepdaan and tulsi puja, and this practice completes on Kartik Purnima. Bhishma Panchak also ends on this day. It is believed that Bhagwan Shiv destroyed Tripurasur on this day, so it is also called Tripurari Purnima. Many traditions also link the story of Bhagwan Vishnu's Matsya avatar to this day. Places like Haridwar, Prayagraj, Varanasi, Garhmukteshwar and Pushkar see huge crowds on this day.

How to take the snan at home

  1. Wake up before sunrise. If you cannot go to a river, mix a little Gangajal into your bathing water.
  2. While bathing, remember Ganga Maa, for example by saying 'Gange cha Yamune chaiva Godavari Saraswati. Narmade Sindhu Kaveri jale'smin sannidhim kuru.'
  3. Wear clean clothes and offer water to Surya Dev from a copper lota.
  4. Offer water to the tulsi and light a diya.
  5. Worship Bhagwan Vishnu and sing the Om Jai Jagdish Hare aarti.

If you bathe in a river, stay close to the bank, do not go into strong currents or deep water, and keep an eye on children and the elderly. Do not put soap or shampoo in the river.

Give according to your means. Do not feel pressured to buy something expensive in the name of a 'special remedy'. What matters most is that the daan reaches someone who really needs it.

Evening deepdaan vidhi

  1. After sunset, fill clay diyas with ghee or sesame oil and add cotton wicks.
  2. Light the first diya before Bhagwan at your puja place.
  3. Then place diyas near the tulsi, on both sides of the main door, in the kitchen and where drinking water is kept.
  4. If a river or pond is nearby, place a diya in a leaf bowl and gently float it on the water.
  5. Also light a diya at a nearby temple. Some homes also follow the tradition of lighting one diya with 365 wicks on this day.
  6. After deepdaan, do the Lakshmi Ji aarti or the Tulsi Mata aarti.

Keep diyas away from wind and curtains. Diyas, ghee, wicks and puja items: Buy puja samagri.

Satyanarayan katha

The Satyanarayan katha is especially important on Purnima, and many homes hold it on Kartik Purnima. Read the full katha here: Satyanarayan Vrat Katha, the samagri list: Satyanarayan puja samagri, and at the end Satyanarayan Ji aarti.

What not to do on this day

  • Stay away from meat, alcohol and tamasic food.
  • Avoid lying, quarrelling and insulting anyone.
  • Do not litter the river or ghat, and do not throw plastic or puja waste into the water.
  • Avoid pushing on crowded ghats and follow the administration's instructions.

Guru Nanak Jayanti and Dev Deepawali in Varanasi also fall on this day: Guru Nanak Jayanti, Dev Deepawali Varanasi. Watch the ghat aarti live: Varanasi Ganga Aarti live.

Frequently asked questions

When should you take the snan on Kartik Purnima 2026?

Before sunrise on November 24, 2026. In New Delhi, Brahma muhurat is roughly from 5:14 am to 6:02, and sunrise is at 6:50.

When does the Kartik Purnima tithi start and end?

From 11:43 pm on November 23, 2026 to 8:24 pm on November 24 (New Delhi).

What should be donated on Kartik Purnima?

Grain, jaggery, sesame, warm clothes or blankets, diyas and ghee, fruit, and money as per your faith.

What if you cannot go to the Ganga?

At home, mix a little Gangajal into your bathing water and bathe before sunrise.

When should deepdaan be done on Kartik Purnima?

In the evening after sunset, while Purnima tithi lasts. In 2026 the tithi lasts until 8:24 pm.

How many diyas should be lit on Kartik Purnima?

There is no fixed number. Do place a diya before Bhagwan, near the tulsi, at the main door and in the temple. Some homes also light a diya with 365 wicks.

Is a fast kept on Kartik Purnima?

Yes, many people fast on this day and listen to the Satyanarayan katha. Follow your family's custom for the fast.

Why is Kartik Purnima called Tripurari Purnima?

It is believed that Bhagwan Shiv destroyed Tripurasur on this day, so he came to be called Tripurari.
