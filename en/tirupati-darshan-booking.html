Tirupati Darshan Guide: ₹300 ticket, free darshan and dress code

You can have Tirupati Balaji darshan through the free Sarva Darshan too, but the queue is long. For darshan at a fixed time, the ₹300 Special Entry Darshan ticket is sold about three months ahead only on TTD's official website ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in or the TTD app, and sells out within minutes. Traditional dress is compulsory for ticketed darshan.

Book only on ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in or TTD's official app. TTD announces in advance when each month's booking will open.

Easy steps to book the ₹300 ticket

  • Create an account on the TTD website with your mobile number, and be logged in and ready before booking opens.
  • Choose ‘Special Entry Darshan’, pick the date and time slot, and fill in each pilgrim's name and ID (Aadhaar).
  • Pay ₹300 per person and download the ticket. Carry the same original ID on the day of darshan.
  • Beware of fake websites: many look-alike websites and WhatsApp numbers sell ‘VIP darshan’ or tickets in this temple's name. Book only through the official website or app given below, and never pay an agent in advance.

Important things before you go

  • Dress code: men in dhoti-kurta or kurta-pyjama; women in saree, half-saree or churidar with dupatta. It is strictly enforced for ticketed and seva darshans.
  • One laddu prasad is given free after every darshan; extra laddus can be bought at the counter.
  • Mobiles and cameras are not allowed inside the temple; Tirumala has counters to deposit your belongings.
  • Tonsure (hair offering) at Kalyanakatta is free.

How to reach

  • Rail: Tirupati station; the Tirumala hill is about 22 km from there, with TTD and APSRTC buses running all the time.
  • Air: Tirupati (Renigunta) airport, about 15 km from Tirupati city.
  • On foot: the Alipiri route (about 3,550 steps) or the Srivari Mettu route (about 2,388 steps, the shorter way).

When is it less crowded

Saturdays and Sundays, Brahmotsavam, Vaikuntha Ekadashi and summer holidays are the most crowded. Weekdays, especially Tuesday and Wednesday, are relatively easier.

Information checked: October 26, 2026. Official website: ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in. Temple timings, fees and rules keep changing — always check the official website before you travel or pay.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book the Tirupati ₹300 darshan ticket?

Only on TTD's website ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in or the TTD app. Tickets are released about three months ahead and sell out fast.

Is there free darshan at Tirupati?

Yes. Yes — Sarva Darshan, SSD tokens and the Divya Darshan for pilgrims on foot are all free.

Where do I get an SSD token?

At TTD token counters in Tirupati city, on showing your ID; it carries your darshan time for the next day.

What is the dress code at Tirupati?

Men in dhoti-kurta or kurta-pyjama, women in saree or churidar with dupatta.

What darshan arrangements are there for the elderly at Tirupati?

There is a separate free arrangement for the elderly above 65 and the disabled, booked on the TTD website.

What is Srivani darshan?

A break darshan given with a donation to the Srivani Trust, ₹10,500 per person in all.

How many steps are there from Alipiri to Tirumala?

About 3,550 steps. The Srivari Mettu route is shorter, about 2,388 steps.

Is the Tirupati laddu free?

One laddu is given free after every darshan; extra laddus can be bought at the counter.

Where can I watch Tirupati Balaji live darshan?

Press ‘start darshan’ at the top of this page — the stream from TTD's own devotional channel SVBC plays right here.
