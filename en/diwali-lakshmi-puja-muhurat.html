Frequently asked questions

What is the auspicious Lakshmi Puja muhurat on Diwali 2026?

In Delhi, from 5:55 pm to 7:50 pm (Vrishabha lagna within Pradosh Kaal). See the table for other cities.

Diwali 2026 Lakshmi Puja time in Mumbai?

In Mumbai, from 6:28 pm to 8:26 pm.

Diwali 2026 Lakshmi Puja time in Patna?

In Patna, from 5:29 pm to 7:25 pm.

Why is Lakshmi Puja done in Pradosh Kaal and Vrishabha lagna?

Pradosh Kaal is the roughly two and a half hours after sunset, considered best for Lakshmi Puja. Vrishabha is a 'fixed lagna' — it is believed that puja in a fixed lagna makes Lakshmi stay in the home.
