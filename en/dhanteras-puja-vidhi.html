Dhanteras Puja Vidhi: Dhanvantari, Kuber and Yam deepdaan

The Dhanteras puja (November 6, 2026) is done in the evening during Pradosh kaal — in Delhi from 5:32 pm to 7:56 PM. Place pictures of Dhanvantari, Kuber and Lakshmi-Ganesh on a chowki, light a diya, place your new purchases in the puja and do the mantra and aarti. Finally, light a four-wick diya for Yamraj outside the house, facing south.

Puja samagri

  • A wooden chowki, red or yellow cloth
  • A picture or idol of Bhagwan Dhanvantari, Kuber and Lakshmi-Ganesh
  • A ghee diya, 13 clay diyas, one four-wick diya (for Yam deepdaan)
  • Roli, akshat (whole rice), turmeric, flowers, dhoop and agarbatti
  • Sweets, fruit, whole coriander, jaggery
  • New purchases — utensils, a coin or jewellery
  • Everything in one place: buy puja samagri

Puja vidhi — step by step

  1. Clean the house before evening and make a rangoli at the main door.
  2. In Pradosh kaal, sit facing north or east. Spread the cloth on the chowki and place the pictures.
  3. First meditate on Ganesh ji, then light the diya and take the sankalp.
  4. Dhanvantari puja: offer roli, akshat and flowers and chant ‘Om Dhanvantaraye Namah’ 11 or 108 times. Pray for the good health of the family.
  5. Kuber puja: place your new purchases and your safe or wallet in the puja, offer roli and akshat and chant the Kuber Mantra.
  6. Lakshmi puja: offer flowers, sweets and whole coriander to Maa Lakshmi.
  7. Do the Dhanvantari aarti and the Kuber aarti, then share the prasad.
  8. Light 13 diyas in the house — at the main door, the kitchen, the tulsi and the water place.

How to do Yam deepdaan

  1. After the puja, take a four-wick diya of dough or clay and fill it with mustard oil.
  2. Place the diya outside the main door, facing south.
  3. Pray to Yamraj for the long life of the family. Mantra and full vidhi: Yam Dipdan Mantra.
  4. Light the diya and come back inside without looking back.

Puja time

Trayodashi tithi runs from 10:31 am, November 6 to 10:49 am, November 7. Do the puja in the evening Pradosh kaal of November 6 — in Delhi from 5:32 to 7:56 PM. Timings for other cities and full details of Dhanteras.

Information checked: October 27, 2026. These are traditions and beliefs; give priority to your own family's customs.

Frequently asked questions

When should the Dhanteras puja be done?

In the evening Pradosh kaal of November 6, 2026 — in Delhi from 5:32 pm to 7:56 PM.

Who is worshipped on Dhanteras?

Bhagwan Dhanvantari (health), Kuber ji (wealth) and Maa Lakshmi, and in the evening a diya is offered for Yamraj.

How many diyas are lit on Dhanteras?

By tradition 13 diyas are lit, plus one four-wick diya for Yam facing south.

In which direction should the Yam deepak be placed?

Outside the main door of the house, facing south.

What is the mantra of Dhanvantari ji?

‘Om Dhanvantaraye Namah’ — it is chanted 11 or 108 times.

Is a fast kept on Dhanteras?

A fast is not required on Dhanteras; the main things are the evening puja and the deepdaan.
