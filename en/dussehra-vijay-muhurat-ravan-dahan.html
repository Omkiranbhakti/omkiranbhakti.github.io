Frequently asked questions

When is Dussehra 2027?

On October 9, 2027, Saturday. Dashami tithi lasts from October 9, 9:03 am to October 10, 11:41 am.

What is the Vijay muhurat on Dussehra 2027?

In Delhi from 2:05 pm to 2:51 pm; in Mumbai from 2:24 pm to 3:11 pm.

At what time is Ravan Dahan on Dussehra 2027?

Ravan Dahan is done in Pradosh Kaal, after sunset — in Delhi after 5:58 pm, in Mumbai after 6:20 pm. Also check the time set by your local committee.

What is Vijay muhurat?

When the day (sunrise to sunset) is divided into 15 equal parts, the 11th part is called Vijay muhurat. On Dussehra it is considered the most auspicious time for Shami puja, Shastra puja and starting new work.
