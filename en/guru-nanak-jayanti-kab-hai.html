When is Guru Nanak Jayanti 2026: Gurpurab date and traditions

Guru Nanak Jayanti, the Prakash Parv of Guru Nanak Dev Ji, will be celebrated on Tuesday, November 24, 2026, the day of Kartik Purnima. It will be roughly the 557th Prakash Parv of Guru Nanak Dev Ji (born 1469 CE). On this day gurdwaras hold the completion of the Akhand Path, kirtan, ardas and langar, and Nagar Kirtans and Prabhat Pheris are taken out in the days before. Check your local gurdwara's programme with its committee's announcement.

Prakash Parv is celebrated every year on Kartik Purnima, so its English calendar date changes every year. In 2027, Kartik Purnima falls on November 14. Full tithi details: When is Kartik Purnima.

Who was Guru Nanak Dev Ji

Guru Nanak Dev Ji was the first Guru of Sikhism. He was born in 1469 CE at Rai Bhoi di Talwandi, which is now called Nankana Sahib in Pakistan. He made long journeys across India and beyond, called the Udasis, and spread the message of one God, equality and service. He spent the last years of his life at Kartarpur, which is now in Pakistan. His bani is recorded in the Guru Granth Sahib.

Three main teachings

  • Naam Japna: remembering God at all times.
  • Kirat Karni: earning a living honestly and through hard work.
  • Vand Chhakna: sharing your earnings and food with others. The tradition of langar comes from this.

The Guru Granth Sahib begins with the Mool Mantar, the bani of Guru Nanak Dev Ji:

Ik Onkar Sat Naam Karta Purakh Nirbhau Nirvair Akaal Moorat Ajooni Saibhang Gur Prasad.

Simple meaning: God is one, His name is truth, He is the creator of all, He fears no one and hates no one, He is beyond time, is not born, exists by Himself, and is realised through the Guru's grace.

How Gurpurab is celebrated

  1. Prabhat Pheri: for a few weeks before the festival, the sangat goes around the neighbourhood early in the morning singing shabads.
  2. Akhand Path: gurdwaras hold a continuous reading of the Guru Granth Sahib lasting about 48 hours, which usually finishes on the morning of Gurpurab.
  3. Nagar Kirtan: one or two days before the festival, a procession led by the Panj Pyare goes out with the Nishan Sahib and the Guru Granth Sahib in a palki. Gatka (Sikh martial art) is also performed.
  4. The day of Gurpurab: kirtan of Asa di Var at amrit vela, followed by katha, kirtan and ardas. Kada prasad is distributed.
  5. Langar: free food is cooked at the gurdwara for everyone. People of every caste and religion sit together in a pangat and eat.
  6. Night diwan: many gurdwaras hold kirtan late into the night, with lights and diyas.

Keep in mind when you visit a gurdwara

  • Cover your head. Carry a handkerchief or dupatta; most gurdwaras also provide head coverings.
  • Leave your shoes at the joda ghar outside and wash your hands and feet before going in.
  • Do not carry tobacco, alcohol or any intoxicant.
  • Sit on the floor in the pangat at langar and take only as much as you can eat.
  • If you like, do seva in the langar: chopping vegetables, washing utensils or serving.

Gurpurab at the Golden Temple

At Sri Harmandir Sahib (the Golden Temple) in Amritsar, Gurpurab brings special decorations, deepmala and fireworks, and the sangat arrives from all over the world. It gets very crowded, so book your hotel early if you plan to go. Have darshan from home: Golden Temple live darshan.

On the same day, Hindu tradition observes the snan and deepdaan of Kartik Purnima: Kartik Purnima snan, daan and deepdaan.

Frequently asked questions

When is Guru Nanak Jayanti 2026?

On Tuesday, November 24, 2026, the day of Kartik Purnima.

Which Prakash Parv of Guru Nanak Dev Ji is it in 2026?

Guru Nanak Dev Ji was born in 1469 CE, so 2026 marks roughly the 557th Prakash Parv.

What other names is Guru Nanak Jayanti known by?

Prakash Parv, Prakash Utsav and Gurpurab (Guruparv).

Where was Guru Nanak Dev Ji born?

At Rai Bhoi di Talwandi, now called Nankana Sahib. This place is now in Pakistan.

What is done on Gurpurab?

Akhand Path, Nagar Kirtan, Prabhat Pheri, kirtan of Asa di Var, ardas, kada prasad and langar.

When is the Nagar Kirtan taken out?

Usually one or two days before Gurpurab. Check the date for your city with the local gurdwara committee.

Why does Guru Nanak Jayanti fall on a different date every year?

Because it is celebrated on Kartik Purnima, which is set by the lunar calendar. In 2027 it falls on November 14.

What are the main teachings of Guru Nanak Dev Ji?

Naam Japna, Kirat Karni and Vand Chhakna: remember God, earn honestly and share what you have.
