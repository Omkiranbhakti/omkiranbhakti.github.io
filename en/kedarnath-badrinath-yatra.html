Kedarnath–Badrinath Yatra Guide: kapat dates, helicopter and registration

In 2026, the doors (kapat) of Kedarnath Dham close on November 11 (Bhai Dooj) and the doors of Badrinath Dham on November 16 at 9:37 pm for the winter. In winter, Baba Kedar is worshipped at the Omkareshwar temple in Ukhimath. The Uttarakhand government's Char Dham registration is compulsory for the yatra, and the Kedarnath helicopter ticket is sold only on IRCTC's HeliYatra website.

The opening date of Kedarnath for the next year is announced on Mahashivratri, and Badrinath's on Basant Panchami.

Registration — the very first step

  • Free registration on the Uttarakhand government website registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in is compulsory for the Char Dham yatra; without it you cannot even book a helicopter.
  • For special pujas and online donations, the Badri-Kedar Temple Committee (BKTC) website is badrinath-kedarnath.gov.in.

Kedarnath helicopter booking

  • Book only on heliyatra.irctc.co.in. 2 tickets per user ID, and up to 6 passengers per ticket.
  • Booking does not open all at once but in phases; the date of each phase is announced in advance.
  • HelipadReturn fare (2026 season)
    SersiAbout ₹6,390
    PhataAbout ₹10,164
    GuptkashiAbout ₹12,762
  • IRCTC's fee is charged on top of the fare, and a fuel surcharge may be added during the season.
  • Beware of fake websites: many look-alike websites and WhatsApp numbers sell ‘VIP darshan’ or tickets in this temple's name. Book only through the official website or app given below, and never pay an agent in advance.

Trek and road

  • Kedarnath: a trek of about 16–18 km from Gaurikund; hire only government-registered ponies, palkis and pithus.
  • Badrinath: a road goes right up to the temple; about 300 km from Rishikesh.
  • The nearest railway station is Rishikesh (Yog Nagari) and the nearest airport is Jolly Grant, Dehradun.

When to go

The doors usually open in April–May and close in October–November. May–June is the most crowded, and the rains of July–August bring a risk of landslides. In September–October the weather is clear and crowds are smaller.

Information checked: October 26, 2026. Official website: badrinath-kedarnath.gov.in. Temple timings, fees and rules keep changing — always check the official website before you travel or pay.

Frequently asked questions

When will the Kedarnath doors close in 2026?

On November 11, 2026, the day of Bhai Dooj.

When will the Badrinath doors close?

On November 16, 2026 at 9:37 pm.

Where is Kedarnath worshipped in winter?

At the Omkareshwar temple in Ukhimath, where Baba Kedar's doli stays for the winter.

Where is Badrinath darshan held in winter?

At Pandukeshwar (Yogadhyan Badri) and the Narsingh temple in Jyotirmath.

Where do I book the Kedarnath helicopter?

Only on IRCTC's website heliyatra.irctc.co.in, with your Char Dham registration number.

What is the Kedarnath helicopter fare?

In the 2026 season the return fare was about ₹6,390 from Sersi, ₹10,164 from Phata and ₹12,762 from Guptkashi, plus IRCTC's fee.

Where is Char Dham registration done?

On the Uttarakhand government website registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in, free of cost.

How far is Kedarnath from Gaurikund?

About 16 to 18 km on foot.

When will Kedarnath open in 2027?

The opening date will be announced on Mahashivratri 2027; the doors usually open in April–May.

Where can I watch Kedarnath live darshan?

Press ‘start darshan’ at the top of this page — the stream from the Badri-Kedar Temple Committee (BKTC) channel plays right here.
