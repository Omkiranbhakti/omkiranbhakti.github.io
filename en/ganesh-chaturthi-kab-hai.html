When is Ganesh Chaturthi?

Ganesh Chaturthi in 2027 falls on September 4, 2027, Saturday. It is observed at midday on the Chaturthi tithi of the Shukla paksha of Bhadrapad month.

Ganesh Chaturthi: date and time

12:26 pm, September 4		Madhyahna (midday): 11:03 am to 1:35 pm

Ganpati sthapana at midday. Its puja is done at midday (madhyahna), so the day is chosen whose midday has this tithi.
